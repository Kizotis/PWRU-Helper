# Story E7.S1: One sentence per error kind, with a countdown

Status: **ready-for-dev (Phase 4 — owner's go given)**

_Epic E7 (G) — Degraded-mode UX · increment 6 · **Release B.2** · the owner's Phase-4 go was given 2026-09-06
(`README.md` § Phase 4)._

> **This is the story E1.S6 was written to be reversed by.** `Services/UserMessages.cs` already exists — GAP-4's
> single table, nine sentences, UI-free, house-rule-tested. What it holds is Sally's deck with **four deliberate
> deviations**, every one of them recorded, ruled on by Winston and marked *"E7.S1 restores the full form"*:
>
> | | Deviation | Why it was taken | What restores it |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | **D1** | **no `{P}`** — no sentence names an engine | naming the answering provider needed `ChainTranslator.LastOutcome`, which did not exist | it does now (E3.S3, `ChainTranslator.cs:101-104`) + **E7.S3**'s provider-name table |
> | **D2** | **no "— trying another engine"** | `Friendly` was only ever reached *after* the whole attempt had already failed; promising a retry that is not happening breaks principle 4 | the sentence now also renders on a **status line above a live chain**, where the promise is true |
> | **D3** | **no `{t}`** in `RateLimited`, `Blocked`, `AllProvidersPaused` | no gate to count down from, and no formatter | **E2** shipped the gate; **E7.S2** ships the formatter |
> | **D4** | **no terminal full stop** | all six call sites *join* the sentence; with the stop the app shipped `…shortly.).` and `. — your text is kept` | §3.1's terminated form for the **standalone** surfaces + `LowerAtJoin` for the joins |
>
> **And D5 is still Sally's open question** — three clauses that deviate beyond D1–D3 (`QuotaExhausted` lost
> *"— using the free engines instead"*; `RateLimited`'s *"try again in a moment"* sits next to the one phrasing
> §3.1's own note bans; `Blocked` answers none of principle 2's three questions). **This story is where it gets
> answered.** If Sally has not, ship §3.1 verbatim — that is the deck, and "verbatim" is a defensible default
> where a reviewer's rewrite is not.

## Story

As a player,
I want one plain sentence that says what happened, when it ends and whether I must do something,
So that I stop reading developer-speak about HTTP responses.

## Acceptance Criteria

_Copied verbatim from `epics.md` § Story E7.S1. Do not weaken._

1. **Given** the `Kind` switch shape landed in E1.S6 (`MainWindow.xaml.cs:404-410`)
   **When** the real copy replaces the placeholders
   **Then** each `Kind` returns exactly the sentence of `ux-mode-degrade.md` §3.1 — `RateLimited`
   (`{P} asked us to slow down — paused for {t}.`), `Blocked`, `Unavailable`, `Timeout`, `Network`, `BadResponse`,
   `QuotaExhausted`, `AuthFailed`, `AllProvidersPaused`
   (`All engines are paused — next try in {t}. Nothing you need to do.`), and **`Cancelled` renders nothing at
   all**.
2. **Given** the copy rules of §3
   **When** any of those strings is reviewed
   **Then** it contains no HTTP status code, no provider internal (`gtx`, `dict-chrome-ex`, `429`), no exclamation
   mark, and it is ≤ 80 characters wherever the overlay can show it.
3. **Given** UX-DR19
   **When** the codebase is searched
   **Then** each user-facing string exists **exactly once** — no ad-hoc re-phrasing at a call site; that is what
   the ten strings E1–E10 cost this project.
4. **Given** a successful fallback
   **When** a lower tier answered
   **Then** **no error text appears anywhere** — the user got their translation; only the chip changes plus the
   one-time notice of §3.5 (UX acceptance hint 5).
5. **Given** I2
   **When** `{t}` is rendered
   **Then** `MainWindow` formats the countdown from `RetryAt`; `Services/` never formats a string for display.

## §3.1, verbatim — the target

| Kind | Sentence | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `RateLimited` | `{P} asked us to slow down — paused for {t}.` | never "wait a minute": we now know the real number |
| `Blocked` | `{P} is refusing requests from your connection — paused for {t}.` | the honest wording for a 403 / captcha page |
| `Unavailable` | `{P} is down right now — trying another engine.` | 5xx |
| `Timeout` | `{P} took too long to answer — trying another engine.` | |
| `Network` | `No internet connection — nothing can be translated until it is back.` | the only kind with no `{P}` |
| `BadResponse` | `{P} sent something we could not read — trying another engine.` | |
| `QuotaExhausted` | `Your {P} free quota is used up — using the free engines instead.` | keys only |
| `AuthFailed` | `Your {P} key was refused — check it in About, or clear it.` | keys only |
| `Cancelled` | *(nothing)* | must never reach a surface |
| `AllProvidersPaused` | `All engines are paused — next try in {t}. Nothing you need to do.` | the single most important line in this document |

`{P}` is the stable user-facing provider name of **OQ-2**: `Google`, `Edge`, `Google (old)`, `DeepL`, `Azure`,
`Offline engine`.

        wording (`the translation service`); no countdown ⇒ the no-`{t}` form. Both are reachable — a

        type as a row of `Sentence(kind)`"* (`UserMessages.cs:164-167`, and E5.S4's Completion Notes hit it).
        Keep the consts as the **no-provider, no-countdown** base forms and compose the parameterised ones from
        them, or the house-rule tests break for a reason that has nothing to do with copy.
- [ ] **T3 — D4: the terminal stop comes back, and the joins get `LowerAtJoin` (AC 1).**
  - [ ] §3.1's sentences are **terminated** — they are written for standalone status lines, which is what E7.S3

  - [ ] The joins that survive get the rule E5.S4's review settled and `UserMessages.cs:276-295` documents:
        **after a colon** the deck sentence is lower-cased at the join (`LowerAtJoin`, with its
        second-capital guard so "GDI+ capture failed" is left alone); **after a full stop** it keeps its own
        capital and is terminated (`Because`/`Terminated`, `:297-308`). Do not re-derive this — read the two
        doc comments first; they carry the arguments.
  - [ ] **`Every_surface_reads_as_a_sentence_once_the_wrapper_is_applied`** (E1.S6's composition test) is the
        test that must be **updated, not deleted**. It composes six wrappers × nine sentences. E7.S5 rewrites
        four of the six wrappers; this story re-terminates the sentence. **Land them in an order where that test
        is green at every commit** — see the release order note.
- [ ] **T4 — D2: "— trying another engine" comes back only where it is TRUE (AC 1, and principle 4).**
      E1.S6's ruling was not squeamishness: `Friendly` is reached **after** the whole chain has failed, so the

  - [ ] **Standalone status line, mid-chain** (E7.S4/E7.S5) — the chain **is** about to try another engine.

## Dev Notes

### Invariants this story must keep

- **I2 — `Services/` never formats a countdown.** AC 5. The deck **receives** rendered text
  (`ReadOncePaused(lines, tryAgainIn)` is the shipped precedent, `:265-268`). No `TimeSpan`, no
  `DateTimeOffset`, no `.ToString("m\\:ss")` anywhere under `Services/`.
- **I4 — never start a sentence with `(`.** `UserMessages.cs:36-44`. `(` is the app's marker for *"this is a
  failure, not a translation"* — what `CachingTranslator.IsCacheable` refuses to store — and the two feed-row
  call sites add the parens. A leading paren here renders `((…))`.
- **I15 — every new `Run.Text` binding is `Mode=OneWay` and gains an STA render case.** This story adds no
  binding (it changes strings), but **if a status line becomes a `Run`, it does.** E7.S6 owns the render-test
  discipline; do not slip a binding in here without one.
- **I3** — untouched, but note the reason `Cancelled` renders nothing is the same reason `Cancelled` may not be
  named: I3's contract lives in `TranslationErrors.cs` and TP-MAP-17 enforces it by source scan.
- **NFR11 — never colour alone**; **NFR12 — no modal from a background path.** Nothing here opens a dialog.

### Rulings that apply

- **GAP-4** — the copy deck is one file (`Services/UserMessages.cs`) so H10/H11 are *tested*, not hoped for.
- **OQ-2** — a stable user-facing provider name. T1.
- **R-3** — `ChainTranslator.LastOutcome` is what carries "who answered / why a higher tier was skipped"
  **without touching `ITranslator`** (I1). Already shipped (E3.S3); this story consumes it through E7.S3.
- **E1.S6's Winston rulings** — D1/D2 stand for A.0 with **E7.S1 restoring the full forms**; D4 added in review
  for the same reason. This story is the restoration.
- **E5-g** — the read-once cancel affordance (T8).

### Repo traps

- **`UserMessagesTests` has house-rule tests that read the type's public `const`s reflectively.** E5.S4's dev run
  hit this: adding parameterised sentences as `const`s broke `Sentences()`. Parameterised copy goes in as
  **methods**; the nine `Kind` rows stay `const`s. `UserMessages.cs:162-167` writes the rule down.
- **Six join sites, and E1.S6's composition test knows all six.** `MainWindow.Translate.cs:106` (`Failed: {s}`),
  the two feed-row `({s})` stamps, `MainWindow.Live.cs:394` and `:411`, and
  `CompactOverlay.xaml.cs:142`'s `⚠ {s} — your text is kept…`. **E7.S5 rewrites four of them.** Changing the
  sentence and the wrapper in two stories means the composition test must be green at each — sequence
  deliberately.
- **`MainWindow.Friendly` is `internal static`** (`MainWindow.xaml.cs:502`) and is the display-time entry point;
  its doc comment already says the countdown *"will be formatted from `TranslationException.RetryAt` when E7.S1
  adds it"*. That is the signature that grows, not `UserMessages.For`.
- **`TranslationException` carries `RetryAt` and `providerId`** — both nullable. Both nulls are reachable
  (`NotSent` refusals, untyped exceptions through `For`'s `_` arm).
- **`LowerAtJoin`'s second-capital guard exists because of a real bug** (`:286-291`): "GDI+ capture failed"
  joined as "gDI+ capture failed". Do not simplify it.
- **The dark `ToolTip` style in `Theme.xaml` is load-bearing** and **`MainTabs_SelectionChanged` keeps its
  `e.Source is TabControl` filter** — epic-level DoD, restated because this is the epic's first story.
- No `.sln`; `InternalsVisibleTo` (`PWRUHelper.csproj:28`) and `DefaultItemExcludes` (`:18`) intact.

  **H1 → TP-MAP-01/TP-MAP-17**, **H5 → §6.3's S2 row** (AC 4: a successful fallback produces no error text
  anywhere), **H10/H11 → GAP-4's source scan**. The epic asks for one case per `Kind`, the `Cancelled` negative,

  already has the shape): no digit-triplet status code; no provider internal (`gtx`, `dict-chrome-ex`, and the

  `PublishFlagsTests` / `ProviderStateStoreTests` file-reading shape is the tool; `Code()` strips comments so a
  quoted sentence in a doc comment does not count as a second occurrence.

  `Every_surface_reads_as_a_sentence_once_the_wrapper_is_applied` — six wrappers × nine sentences, no `.).`, no
  `. —`, no `((`.

  no-provider fallback; for each of the three `{t}`-bearing rows, the countdown form **and** the

  fallback.

- **Must keep passing unchanged:** `UserMessagesTests`' existing rows and house rules, `ReadOnceStatusTests`

## How to verify manually

1. `dotnet test tests/PWRUHelper.Tests` — green.
2. **`{P}` is real.** Force a Google 429 with a key saved: the sentence names **Google**, not "the translation
   service", and never `google-dict`.
3. **`{t}` is real and it counts.** Block a provider for two minutes: the status reads
   `Google asked us to slow down — paused for about 2 min.` and steps at 1 Hz per §2.4's bands (E7.S2).
4. **AC 4, the silent success.** Block Google only: the feed keeps filling from the next tier and **no error text
   appears anywhere** — only the chip changes.
5. **`AuthFailed` no longer invites a retry.** Save a wrong key: the sentence says *check it in About, or clear
   it* — not "try again shortly", which is what it says today over a pause that no timer ends.
6. **The joins still read as sentences.** Overlay quick reply on a failure: `⚠ … — your text is kept, press
   Enter to retry.` — one stop, in the right place.
