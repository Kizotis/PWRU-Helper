(`README.md` § Phase 4)._

> **The copy pass over everything E3 and E5 shipped provisionally.** Six story files say, in their own comments,
> that their wording is a placeholder for this story: `LivePausedStatus`' doc comment (*"the wording is
> provisional in Sally's §3.2 shape"*), `UserMessages.PendingRetryRow` (*"E7.S1 owns the final copy"*),

>
> **Read "E7.S1" in all of those as "E7.S1 for §3.1, this story for §3.2–§3.5."** E7.S1 owns the nine
> `Kind` sentences; this story owns the LIVE statuses, the read-once statuses, the overlay's quick reply and the
> fallback notice — the four sections of the deck that *join* E7.S1's sentences rather than being them.
>
> **And it lands the one sentence the app currently gets most wrong**: §3.5's fallback notice. Today a
> successful fallback is completely silent (which is right — UX hint 5) but the chip changes with no
> explanation. `Translated by Edge — Google is paused.` **once per switch** is what makes the chip's change read
> as information rather than as a glitch.

## Story

As a player,
I want every status line to tell me the reason and the way back,
So that a stopped feature never leaves me guessing what to press.

## Acceptance Criteria

_Copied verbatim from `epics.md` § Story E7.S5. Do not weaken._

1. **Given** UX-DR8
   **When** LIVE changes state
   **Then** the seven strings of §3.2 replace `MainWindow.Live.cs:235` and `:238`, including
   `🔴 Live — back on. Catching up on {n} message(s).` on recovery and the two honest auto-stop sentences.
2. **Given** UX-DR9
   **When** a read-once completes
   **Then** the five strings of §3.3 replace `MainWindow.Ocr.cs:243-244`, and `Done — {n} line(s) translated.`
   appears **only** when every line has a translation; `Could not read the screen: {reason}` replaces today's
   developer-speak `OCR failed:` prefix.
3. **Given** UX-DR10
   **When** the compact overlay's quick reply fails
   **Then** the four strings of §3.4 replace `CompactOverlay.xaml.cs:142`, each under ~60 characters, and the
   existing promise **"your text is kept"** is preserved **verbatim** wherever it still applies.
4. **Given** UX-DR11
   **When** the chain switches tiers
   **Then** the notice (`Translated by Edge — Google is paused.` / `Translated by the offline engine — no
   internet needed.` / `Back on Google.`) is shown **once per switch** in the status line — never per row, never
   per message, never as a toast.
5. **Given** `{reason}` in §3.3
   **When** it is joined into a sentence
   **Then** it is the §3.1 sentence lower-cased at the join.

## The four sections, verbatim

**§3.2 — LIVE statuses** (seven):

| Situation | String |
|---|---|
| running (unchanged) | `🔴 Live — watching (check #{n})…` |
| one failed read, still trying | `🔴 Live — one read did not translate, retrying…` |
| the preferred engine is paused, a backup serves | `🔴 Live — {P} paused ({t}), using {P2}.` |
| everything paused | `○ Live — paused, next try in {t}. It resumes on its own; nothing is lost.` |
| recovered | `🔴 Live — back on. Catching up on {n} message(s).` |
| auto-stopped, honestly | `Live stopped — {n} reads in a row failed. Press ▶ to try again.` |
| auto-stopped because everything is paused past the cap | `Live stopped — every engine is paused. Try again in {t}, or add the offline engine in About.` |

**§3.3 — Read-once** (five): `Done — {n} line(s) translated.` ·
`Read {n} line(s) — {k} translated, {m} could not be. {reason}` ·
`Read {n} line(s) — none could be translated. {reason}` ·
`Read {n} line(s) — all engines are paused. They will fill in when one is back.` ·
`Could not read the screen: {reason}`

**§3.4 — Overlay quick reply** (four, **under ~60 characters** — the overlay is 360 px wide):
`⚠ {reason} — your text is kept, press Enter to retry.` · `⚠ Engines paused ({t}) — your text is kept.` ·
`⚠ No internet — your text is kept, press Enter to retry.` · `⚠ Your {P} key was refused — see About.`

**§3.5 — Fallback notice** (three, once per switch, status line only):
`Translated by Edge — Google is paused.` · `Translated by the offline engine — no internet needed.` ·
`Back on Google.`

### Rulings that apply

- **E5-d** — no time window; 5 consecutive **sent** failures. **§3.2's seventh string is unreachable under it**
  (T1).

  off. So `Live hiccup (…) — retrying…` (`MainWindow.Live.cs:411`'s neighbourhood) must not be written for a
  refusal.
- **E5-g** — read-once always captures and OCRs; the pause is *reported* from the chain, so `{n}` is known
  (T2), and the cancel affordance is E7's (T8).
- **E2-d** — the three per-line placeholder strings belong to **E3.S8** (batch policy) and **E7.S1** (copy);
  E3.S8 shipped them, this pass owns their wording. They are **row** text, `(`-wrapped by the call site.
- **E3-d** — Edge is not shipped; §3.5 and §3.2 must be parameterised, not literal.
- **R-3** — `LastOutcome` supplies `{P}`, `{P2}` and the skip reason.
