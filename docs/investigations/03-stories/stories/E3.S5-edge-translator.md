# Story E3.S5: `EdgeTranslator` as the independent second vendor

Status: **blocked — depends on E3.S2 (the U2 capture)**

_Epic E3 (C) — Provider chain and the new default endpoint · increment 2 · **Release A.1: E2 and E3 are separate
PRs but ONE release** · the owner's Phase-4 go was given 2026-09-06 (`README.md` § Phase 4)._

> **Hard gate, stated twice in the source documents and repeated here.** `architecture-cible.md` §7.2: *"No
> `EdgeTranslator` code is written before that capture exists."* `test-plan.md` TP-PRV-06: *"this case and
> `EdgeTranslator` do not exist until the capture does — fixture presence is itself the gate."* `epics.md` E3.S5
> AC 4: *"Given E3.S2 returned no-go, this story is **cancelled**, not guessed."*
>
> **Do not start this story on the strength of the presumed shapes in §7.2.** They are marked [UNKNOWN] and are
> labelled *presumed, unverified* in the architecture itself. A provider written against a guessed body does not
> fail at build time — it fails in the field as a tier that takes three `BadResponse` strikes and stays paused,
> which is worse than not having the tier.
>
> **This story may also end as `cancelled`.** That is a valid outcome, not a failure. See "If E3.S2 says no-go".

## Story

As a player,
I want a fallback that is not Google,
So that one vendor's decision cannot take the whole feature away.

## Acceptance Criteria

_Copied verbatim from `epics.md` § Story E3.S5. Do not weaken._

1. **Given** E3.S2 produced a capture
   **When** `EdgeTranslator` is written
   **Then** it matches the captured body, response shape and headers **exactly**, and the capture is the parser's
   test fixture.
2. **Given** the endpoint of §7.2
   **Then** it is
   `POST https://edge.microsoft.com/translate/translatetext?from={src or empty}&to={tgt}&isEnterpriseClient=false`,
   keyless, with auto-detect obtained by omitting `from`.
3. **Given** the error mapping of §7.2
   **Then** 429 → `RateLimited`; 401/403 → `Blocked` (no key is sent, so never `AuthFailed`); 5xx → `Unavailable`;
   non-JSON → §4.3 then `BadResponse`.
4. **Given** E3.S2 returned no-go
   **When** this story is reached
   **Then** it is **cancelled**, not guessed.

## Dev Notes

### Invariants this story must keep

- **I1** — `ITranslator` unchanged; the priority rides on the instance (T1).
- **I2** — `Services/`, UI-free.
- **I3** — no OCE catch in this file. The core's is the only one on the request path.
- **I5** — never pad (T4).
- **I7** — the caller chooses the source per message. This provider receives `"ru"` or `"auto"` and translates
  what it is given; if the response carries a detected language, it is **discarded**, exactly as `GoogleDict`
  discards shape B's second element.
- **I11** — no user text, no full URL in the log.

### Why this tier is worth the trouble at all

It is the only thing in the whole design that makes the free path **not all Google**. R1 says `dict-chrome-ex` is
undocumented and rented and could be blocked next; `gtx` already was, in a fortnight. Three free tiers where two
are the same vendor is one vendor decision away from zero. That is the entire argument, and it is also why the
"cancelled" outcome must be recorded as a **risk increase** rather than as a tidy scope cut.

### Repo traps

- **`HttpSeamGuardTests` finds providers by reflection.** Ctor shape and field names, as in E3.S4's T1.
- **`FakeHandler` repeats its last step** (`FakeHandler.cs:103`).
- **`ProviderIds.Edge` already exists** (`ProviderGates.cs:18`) and `ProviderIds.All` already lists it — E2.S2
  shipped the whole id table ahead of the providers. Do not add an id; use it.
- Do not touch `MainWindow.*` — wiring is **E3.S7's**.
- `InternalsVisibleTo` / `DefaultItemExcludes` intact, **no `.sln`**.
