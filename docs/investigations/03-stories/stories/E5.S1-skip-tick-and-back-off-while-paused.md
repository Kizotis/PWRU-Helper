# Story E5.S1: Skip the whole tick and back off while every read tier is paused

Status: **ready-for-dev (Phase 4 — owner's go given)**

_Epic E5 (E) — Honest live translation · increment 4 · **Release A.2 (E4 + E5)** · the owner's Phase-4 go was
given 2026-09-06 (`README.md` § Phase 4)._

> **This is the story that changes what a player sees.** Everything E1–E4 shipped is invisible by design; this one
> takes the app's most-watched loop and makes it stop.
>
> **It also retires an escalation the project accepted with its eyes open.** E2.S5's record: *"Escalation accepted
> for A.1: no network → LIVE auto-stops after ~3 s (5 refused ticks inside the 5 s cooldown) — same outcome as A.0
> with zero requests sent; **E5.S1 replaces it with the paused state**."* That sentence is this story's real
> acceptance criterion, and it is why the no-network case belongs here and not in a UX story.
>
> **It is the subtlest loop in the app.** I16 lists eleven behaviours that must not move. `LiveDedup` is not
> touched — at all — and that is a task, not a hope.

## Story

As a player mid-raid,
I want a paused app to cost nothing,
So that a translation outage never becomes a frame-rate problem.

## Acceptance Criteria

_Copied verbatim from `epics.md` § Story E5.S1. Do not weaken._

1. **Given** the LIVE loop (`MainWindow.Live.cs:179-245`) and the capture at `:187`
   **When** every tier of the read chain reports `Open`
   **Then** the **entire tick body is skipped** — no capture, no OCR, **no `LiveDedup.Next`**, no request — the
   status shows a countdown on the main window **and** the overlay, `backoffSteps` increments, and the wait becomes
   `min(CurrentLiveIntervalMs() << backoffSteps, LiveBackoffCapMs)` (×2, capped at 5 s).
2. **Given** **OQ-B, settled by the owner: "No — full pause"**
   **When** a tick is skipped
   **Then** nothing is captured and nothing is OCR'd — the feed freezes with a countdown, at zero CPU, and there is
   no retry queue of un-OCR'd frames to bound.
3. **Given** `LiveDedup.Next` is never called during the pause (`LiveDedup.cs:91-97`)
   **When** the gate closes
   **Then** a message still on screen is genuinely fresh and gets translated, and `LiveDedup`'s internal `_tick`
   has not advanced, so `ReappearAfterFrames` did not expire during the pause.
4. **Given** the first tick that **translated successfully**
   **When** it completes
   **Then** `backoffSteps` resets to 0.
5. **Given** `DefaultsAndResizeTests.cs:16,28-32` pins `LiveSpeedPercent = 92` and the 700/3000/500 mapping
   **When** the back-off lands
   **Then** it changes the **wait between ticks**, not the pure function `LiveIntervalMs(double)`, and those pins
   stay green.

## Two things the ACs do not say, and one they say imprecisely

**1. How the loop learns that "every tier reports Open" — without naming `ProviderGates`.**
`ProviderStateStoreTests.No_startup_path_mentions_ProviderGates` (TP-START-02) asserts, as an **exact-equality
assert on a one-element array**, that `MainWindow.xaml.cs: ProviderGates.Flush();` is the *only* reference to the
registry outside `Services/` in the whole app. So the loop may not ask the registry. It asks **the chain it
already holds**:

- `ChainTranslator` gains `internal ChainPause PauseNow()` → `record ChainPause(bool AllPaused, DateTimeOffset? RetryAt)`.
  It walks `_tiers`, reads `tier.Gate.Snapshot()` and compares `BlockedUntil` against `tier.Gate.Now()` — **the
  same two lines `RunAsync` already runs at `ChainTranslator.cs:177-182`**, extracted so both callers share one
  definition of "paused". `AllPaused` is true when every tier is blocked; `RetryAt` is the **earliest**
  `BlockedUntil` (the `Earliest` helper at `:278-280` — the chain gets a rung back when the *first* tier reopens).
- It is **side-effect free by contract**: `Snapshot()` may not advance a clock, take the probe or spend a token
  (ruling R-2). Never call `TryEnter` for a status read.
- The code-behind needs a `ChainTranslator`, not an `ITranslator`. `TranslationChains.BuildRead` already *returns*
  `ChainTranslator.Of(...)`; widen its declared return type to `ChainTranslator` and keep the chain in a new
  `private readonly ChainTranslator _readChain;` alongside `_readTranslator` (the decorator, which is still what
  translates). **E7.S3 needs the same reference for `LastOutcome`** — this is the field that serves both.

**2. Ruling E5-a is already half-implemented, and the half that is left is a NEGATIVE requirement.**
*"LIVE honours short `Wait`s (≤ 1 s) inside the tick … A `Background` caller still needs its own fall-through when
`Delay` exceeds the tick budget."* `HttpProviderCore` (`:362-376`) already waits at most `MaxWaits` (2) times,
each `WorthWaiting` and cumulatively bounded by `MaxSpacingWaitMs`, and raises `Paused` otherwise. So the loop's
job is **not to pre-empt it**: a rate-ceiling wait does **not** set `BlockedUntil`, so `PauseNow()` does not see
it, so the tick runs and the core waits inside it. Write that down — the wrong "fix" here is a pre-tick check that
also treats a token-bucket wait as an outage, which would pause LIVE for a condition that clears in 500 ms.

**3. "every tier reports `Open`" is imprecise, and ruling E3-a already settled the precise form.**
Skip on **`BlockedUntil > Now()`**, never on `State == Open` — that state deliberately outlives its window, and a
`State`-based check ships R-01 (a permanently paused app) silently. `ChainTranslator.RunAsync` does it right at
`:177-182`; `PauseNow()` must do exactly the same thing.

## What "paused" covers — including no network (GAP-3)

`Network` classifies to `GateReaction.SoftCooldown`, which calls `BlockUntil(now + SoftCooldownSecs)`
(`ProviderGate.cs:612-616`). So a machine with no connection has **every read tier blocked for 5 s at a time**, and
`PauseNow().AllPaused` is true — the full pause applies to UX state **S6** with no special case. That is ruling
**GAP-3 / R-6**: *"full pause is universal"*, and it is why the loop stops capturing when the cable is out.

**This directly contradicts `ux-mode-degrade.md` §5 flow (e) step 3** (*"LIVE does not auto-stop for this: it keeps
reading the screen (OCR is local and free) and retries the network on a slow cadence"*). **The ruling wins.** Sally's
S6 text is amended in the stories, as the ruling says. Nothing captures, nothing OCRs; the loop re-checks every
≤ 5 s, so "the moment the connection returns the feed catches up" is still true — it just costs zero CPU while it
waits.

## Dev Notes

### Invariants this story must keep

- **I16 — the eleven LIVE behaviours that must not move.** The three dedup layers, the ru/auto batch join, the
  `rateLimited` latch, the strictly sequential loop, the token reaching `HttpClient`. This story adds a branch
  *in front of* the tick; it changes nothing inside one.
- **I3 — the OCE trap.** `Live.cs:227`'s `catch (OperationCanceledException) when (ct.IsCancellationRequested)`
  is the guard, and the comment above it (`:221-226`) is the best explanation of the trap in the repo. **Any new
  `await` this story adds inside the loop is inside that guard's scope** — a timeout must keep falling through to
  the generic handler. TP-LIVE-16 pins it; do not touch `:227` or `:243-244`.
- **I4** — nothing is cached on a skipped tick because nothing is translated.
- **I10 / TP-START-02** — the code-behind still names no `ProviderGates`.
- **I2** — `LiveTickPolicy` and `ChainTranslator.PauseNow` are UI-free.

### Rulings that apply

- **OQ-B (owner): full pause.** No capture, no OCR. Not negotiable and not an optimisation — it is the product's
  first requirement (nothing may lag the game) and it is what makes the burned-row class go away for free.
- **GAP-3 / R-6: full pause is universal**, S6 included. See the section above; `ux-mode-degrade.md` §5 flow (e)
  step 3 is superseded.
- **E3-a**: skip on `BlockedUntil > Now()`, never on `State == Open`.
- **R-2 / OQ-c**: the UI polls `Snapshot()`; `Services/` emits no events. `PauseNow()` is a poll, and the 1 Hz
  timer that will call it for the chip is **E7.S2**.
- **E5-a**: short `Wait`s are honoured **inside** the tick, by `HttpProviderCore`. The loop must not pre-empt them.
- **E3-h**: lines beyond `PerLineCap` are marked failed and re-translated after recovery **by E5** — that is
  **E5.S3**, and it depends on this story.
- **E2.S5's accepted escalation** (no network ⇒ auto-stop in ~3 s) **is retired here**. Say so in the PR: it is a
  user-visible behaviour change and it was recorded as temporary.

### Repo traps

- **`_liveTicks` is the heartbeat AND the user-visible check number** (`:184-185`, `:209`, `:216-217`). A skipped
  tick should not advance the "check #n" the player reads as progress; decide once, comment it, and keep the
  status strings consistent with the decision.
- **`SetScreenStatus` writes two surfaces** (`:157-161`) — use it and AC 1's "main window and the overlay" is free.
  `SetLiveUi` (`:163-173`) resets `LiveIndicator.Text` to `"●  LIVE"` on every call; do not call it from the loop.
- **The overlay's blink is its own `DispatcherTimer`** (`CompactOverlay.xaml.cs:46-51`), not driven by the loop.
  E7.S4 owns freezing it. Do not reach into the overlay from `LiveLoop`.
- **`ChainCompositionTests.cs:320-322` pins the ctor line as text** and will fail with a message about
  `CachingTranslator`. Update it deliberately (T3).
- **`InitializeComponent()` fires change handlers** — the new `_readChain` assignment goes **above** `:86`, like
  the three it joins. `_restoringSettings` is not engaged: this story adds no control and persists no setting.
- **The suite must stay headless.** TP-LIVE-01/15 need a fake capture/OCR/translator triple, not a screen. If the
  loop cannot be driven headlessly, that is a seam to extract — **not** a reason to make the test STA.
- No `.sln`; `InternalsVisibleTo` (`PWRUHelper.csproj:28`) and `DefaultItemExcludes` (`:18`) intact.

## Size, dependencies, risk

**Size:** M · **Depends on:** **E3.S3** (`ChainTranslator`), E3.S7 (the chains in the ctor). Not on E4 —
though landing E4 first makes E5.S3's drain nearly free ·
**Blocks:** **E5.S2, E5.S3** (both `Depends on: E5.S1`), **E7.S2/E7.S4** (the countdown and the frozen heartbeat) ·
**Risk:** **medium — this is the subtlest loop in the app.** Three specific ways to get it wrong: (a) skipping on
`State == Open` instead of `BlockedUntil` ⇒ R-01, an app that is correctly paused for ever; (b) treating a
rate-ceiling `Wait` as a pause ⇒ LIVE stops for a condition that clears in 500 ms; (c) touching `LiveDedup`
"while we're here" ⇒ the one guarantee AC 3 rests on ·
**DoD:** zero requests, zero OCR calls and a visible countdown under a forced-open gate.

## How to verify manually

1. `dotnet test tests/PWRUHelper.Tests` — green, `LiveDedupTests`/`LiveDefaultsTests`/`DefaultsAndResizeTests`
   included and unedited.
2. Hand-edit `%AppData%\PWRUHelper\provider-state.json` to block **every** read provider (`google-dict`,
   `google-gtx`) 10 minutes into the future. Restart, start LIVE over the game chat.
   - The feed **freezes**; no new rows appear.
   - The status on the Translator tab **and** in the compact overlay says paused, with a time.
   - Task Manager: the app's CPU is **flat** — no capture, no OCR. (Before this story it OCR'd every 700 ms.)
   - The log shows **no** `tr` lines and no `gate` lines.
3. Watch the tick spacing in the log or with a stopwatch: 0.7 s → 1.4 → 2.8 → 5 → 5 → 5…
4. Restore the file (or wait the window out), and let the gate close: the next tick translates, the feed resumes,
   and the spacing goes straight back to 0.7 s.
5. **Messages that were on screen throughout the pause get translated when it ends** — that is AC 3, and it is the
   behaviour that used to burn those rows for the session.
6. **Pull the network cable** with LIVE running: the loop pauses (S6) instead of auto-stopping after ~3 s. Plug it
   back in: the feed catches up within ≤ 5 s, with no button pressed. That is E2.S5's escalation, retired.
