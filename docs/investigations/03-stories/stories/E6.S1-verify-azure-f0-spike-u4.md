# Story E6.S1: Verify the Azure F0 free tier on a real resource 🔬 spike (U4)

Status: **blocked-owner (Phase 4 — needs a real Azure account)**

_Epic E6 (F) — Bring your own key: Azure · increment 5 · **Release B.1** · the owner's Phase-4 go was given
2026-09-06 (`README.md` § Phase 4). This story is **not** blocked by any code: it blocks the **copy**, and only
the copy._

> **What is actually unknown.** `benchmark-fournisseurs.md` §5.4 records F0 as *"2 million characters of any
> combination of standard translation and custom translation training free per month"* with **no duration limit
> stated on the pricing page** — that is **[CONFIRMED]** from the page's own wording — and a Microsoft Q&A that
> describes it as permanent rather than a 12-month trial, which is **[REPORTED]**. Nobody has ever created an F0
> resource and read its portal quota page. Amazon Translate's free tier is *"2 million characters per month for
> **12 months**"* (§5.5) — the identical headline number with an expiry — which is exactly why a page's silence
> about duration is not evidence of permanence.
>
> **What it gates.** One sentence: *"Azure gives you 2 million characters a month for free"* (`ux-mode-degrade.md`
> §4.2, and the cost hint of E6.S4's opt-in), plus *"resets on the 1st"* in §3.7's quota row. Nothing else.
> **`AzureTranslator` (E6.S2), the settings (E6.S3), the opt-in (E6.S4) and the key test (E6.S5) all ship
> regardless** — a provider does not need to know the shape of its free tier to send a request.

## Story

As the owner,
I want to know whether "2 million characters a month, free" is permanent,
So that the app never promises a user something Microsoft can withdraw next quarter.

_Copied verbatim from `epics.md` § Story E6.S1. Do not weaken._

   **Then** the exact wording of the quota, its reset date and any trial expiry are recorded in this folder with a

**Exit criterion (measurable).** A yes/no on "2 M characters per month, no expiry", plus the reset day of the
month.

**Go / no-go.** **Go:** permanent ⇒ the settings copy may say *"Azure gives you 2 million characters a month for
free"* (`ux-mode-degrade.md` §4.2) and *"resets on the 1st"* (§3.7). **No-go:** trial-limited ⇒ the copy is
rewritten to state the limit and its expiry **before** E6.S3 ships; the provider still ships.

### The "20 to 40 hours" arithmetic is *separately* assumed, and this spike does not settle it

`ux-mode-degrade.md` §4.3 derives it: `benchmark…` §8.1 puts a heavy LIVE user at ~48 k characters per hour of
busy chat; F0 is 2 M/month → ≈41 h; a billing multiplier of ×1.3–2.0 (two groups per tick, retries, two caches)
brings it to **≈20–40 h**. §4.3 adds: *"If Winston merges the `ru`/`auto` groups and shares the cache, the
multiplier drops and this sentence must be re-derived, not left to rot."*

**E4 shipped the shared cache.** One store now serves the read chain, the read-once chain and the write chain
(E4.S4), which removes the "two caches" term of that multiplier. The `ru`/`auto` merge was *evaluated and
rejected with arithmetic* (README, Phase 2 closure), so that term stands. **E6.S4 must re-derive the range before
it writes the sentence** — that is E6.S4's task, flagged here because this is the story a reader arrives at
looking for the number.
