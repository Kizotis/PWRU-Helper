### Completion Notes List

**What landed.** `Friendly` is now one line — `internal static string Friendly(Exception ex) => UserMessages.For(ex);`
(`MainWindow.xaml.cs:420`) — and the §4.4 switch lives in the new `Services/UserMessages.cs`: nine `const`
sentences, `Sentence(TranslationErrorKind) → string?` and `For(Exception) → string`. The four arms of the old
switch keep their order and their default; what changed is that a `TranslationException` is rendered from its
`Kind` instead of from whichever sentence the throw site happened to carry.

**Why the scope is not the one written above.** Winston's Phase-4 direction, on the back of E1.S3's review
finding *"E1.S6 rewords against a different baseline than recorded"*. Two facts made the original plan
unbuyable: (1) the empty diff AC 3 asks for was already impossible — `3ec06be` changed three rendered sentences
without changing a literal, so "no user-visible string changed" could not be honestly claimed either way; and
(2) keeping `te.Message` as the arm for most kinds means one dead network still reads three ways
("Couldn't reach the translation service…", "Couldn't reach DeepL…", "no Internet connection") depending on
which provider noticed it first, which is the exact defect the `Kind` vocabulary exists to end. Doing the
reword here also means GAP-4's table exists before E2 starts adding states to it, instead of after.

**Deviations from Sally's §3.1 deck, both deliberate and both narrow.**

- **D1 — no `{P}`.** Every sentence says "the translation service" instead of naming the engine. Naming the
  answering provider needs `ChainTranslator.LastOutcome` (E4/E6/E7); guessing it here would be the same class
  of lie the epic is retiring. `QuotaExhausted` and `AuthFailed` therefore lose the word "DeepL" they had — the
  About tab holds exactly one key today, so nothing is ambiguous, and E6 (Azure) is what makes `{P}` necessary
  rather than decorative.
- **D2 — no "— trying another engine".** `Friendly` is only ever reached **after the whole attempt has already
  failed**; promising a retry that is not happening breaks §1 principle 4 ("honest status only"). Sally's
  first clause is kept verbatim and the trailing promise becomes "— try again shortly". When the sentence moves
  to the status line of a live chain (E7.S1), the promise becomes true and should come back.
- **D3 — no `{t}`.** No countdown: `RetryAt` is formatted by `MainWindow` at display time (I2, §4.4), and
  there is no gate to count down from until E2. `AllProvidersPaused` gets the no-countdown placeholder and an
  `E7.S1 replaces this` marker; nothing raises that `Kind` yet.
